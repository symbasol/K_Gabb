Swimming

How is swimming useful?

Swimming is a highly efficient, full-body exercise that engages nearly all major muscle groups. It elevates your heart rate and builds endurance while remaining incredibly gentle on your joints, making it the perfect workout for all ages and fitness levels.



Why do I swim?

I started swimming back when I was around 5 years old. Since then, I have been swimming, mastered all the strokes efficiently while attending a swimming course. I started swimming because I liked — and still like — swimming and it is generally a good skill to learn.



How has it impacted me?

It helped me keep consistency in attendance, learn strokes, interact with other students, build upper core and lower body strength, build lung capacity, and build endurance by swimming 1km+ every session.

Where do I aim?

I currently aim for professional swimming, though that is not my dream job. I'm currently training/practicing in a swimming club. Hopefully I can reach national or even international level competition and come up in the Olympics.
