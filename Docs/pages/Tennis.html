Tennis

How did it start?

While watching the 2020 Tokyo Summer Olympics, I got inspired by many athletes about the beauty of tennis. Unlike badminton, which needs constant explosive power and quick reflexes, tennis needs endurance and power, which I could gain by simultaneously swimming.

Where have I reached?

I did not join any clubs or anything, just playing with my family and friends, though I improved over the years.

Where do I aim?

For now I don't have the big dream of becoming a world athlete at tennis but if I have the opportunity I'll probably take it.
