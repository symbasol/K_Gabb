Saxophone

How did it grab my attention?

I heard there was a school ensemble for music. Me and my friend wanted to join. We were told to choose our instruments, and we choose the Alto Saxophone.


For 2 years (since grade 5), I have learned how to play the saxophone and have played many songs including Mission Impossible. But unfortunately, when I graduated from elementary, I couldn't carry this musical marvel with me, so I continue to anticipate getting it one day.
