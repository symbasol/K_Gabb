Piano

When did I start?

I started playing the piano at the age of 2.

Why did I start?

I started playing piano because I had the opportunity to learn it, and there was also a piano in the house and I was interested in it.

How did it benefit me?

It has benefited me in several ways, teaching me better hand-eye coordination and musical sense. It also allowed me to stay calm during complex times in a song and even outside the piano.

What songs can I play?

I have learned countless songs, but I can play:

  • Maple Leaf Rag
  • The Entertainer
  • CakeWalk
  • Turkish March
